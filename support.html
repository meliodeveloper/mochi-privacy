About Mochi

Mochi is a calmer way to end your day. It learns how you sleep and builds a gentle nightly wind-down around you — a reflection, a calming exercise, a companion, and on-device insights. It's privacy-first: your journal is end-to-end encrypted, your AI insights are generated on your device, and there's no tracking or advertising.

Common questions

How do I connect Apple Health or my calendar?

Open Settings → Integrations in Mochi and choose Apple Health or Calendar. Both are optional, and you can review or revoke access any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security.

How do I dictate a reflection?

In a new Reflect entry, tap the microphone button. Your speech is transcribed to text entirely on your device — nothing is recorded or sent anywhere. The first time, iOS will ask for microphone and speech-recognition permission.

What is Cloud AI, and is it private?

By default, Mochi's reflections are generated on your device. You can optionally turn on a deeper reflection powered by Claude in Settings → AI. It's off until you enable it, and only the entries you choose are sent (securely) — never your Apple Health data.

How do I change my sleep schedule or reminders?

Your target wake/sleep times and wind-down timing are in Settings → Sleep schedule. Reminders live in Settings → Notifications & reminders.

How do I retake the quiz or change my companion?

Both are at the top of Settings — "Retake archetype quiz" and "Change companion". Retaking the quiz keeps your XP, level, and logs.

How do I delete my account or data?

Go to Settings → Account → Delete account. This permanently removes your account and all its data and can't be undone.
